# GOLF MEOWDYSSEY

Golf Meowdyssey is a 2D pixel art golf platformer. Where our main character, who is a cat, one day wakes up in limbo and finds out it ran out of lives. Desperate about coming back to life to be with its owner, it challenges the Limbo God to a golf game. But not everything's easy, you only have a limited amount of shots, so you better plan every move you make, or else you'll be stuck in Limbo.

This project was built as hands-on training for an entry-level role, applying professional-grade architecture and best practices from day one. Following SOLID principles in order to have a clean, maintainable, stable and robust code. Mentored and reviewed by the talented [Ivan Herrera](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ivan-herrera-m/). Available on [itch.io](https://elerizio.itch.io/golf-meowdyssey).

## Game Features

- Phased shot system: adjust your direction, pick your strenght and shoot. Aim carefully as the force bar fills and empties to land the perfect shot. But be careful, your shots are limited.
- Free camera controls: Free camera controls: move the camera before you make your shot to explore the level and plan your move.
- Power-ups: collect and use power-ups granted by cat spirits in each stage to overcome new challenges.
- Charming dialogues: follow a fun and mischievous narrative through in-game dialogues.
- Level variety: play across different themed stages, offering new puzzles.

## Project Features

- Pull-requests: the code was reviewed first to be approved through pull-requests.
- Project Management: Kanban methodology using GitHub Projects.
- Weekly meetings: meetings were held every week to asign tasks, plan objectives and give feedback about previous tasks.
